## The 4A Framework [stated 2026-11-11]

Leah's consulting methodology for business infrastructure work. Four stages, in order:

1. **Attune**
2. **Assess**
3. **Architect**
4. **Anchor**

Context:

- This is the full methodology, not one of several. Every Salted Honeycomb Collective consulting engagement runs on it.
- It is the methodology behind saltedhoneycombcollective.com, Leah's business infrastructure consulting brand, which funnels into Leah R. Hairston & Associates as the parent brand.
- The work it structures: helping founders (solo service providers and agency owners with teams of 1-5, pushing toward $1M revenue) get out of the parts of the business that were never their strength, by building them the same kind of operational infrastructure and AI agent system Leah runs her own company on (The Hive Office).
- The promise it delivers: clients leave and don't come back with the same problem. They only return if the business has expanded or they need to train someone new.
- Always present the stages as Attune, Assess, Architect, Anchor, in that order.
- Detailed written definitions of each stage have not been supplied yet (as of Nov 11, 2026). When Leah supplies them, add them here and to her main memory.
